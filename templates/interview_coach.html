QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Interview Coach? ▼

AI Interview Coach is an AI-based interview practice solution that conducts realistic mock interview sessions and provides structured feedback to help candidates improve performance.

How does the mock interview work? ▼

Select your interview type (General, Technical, Behavioral, HR), and the AI interviewer will present questions and record your responses for analysis.

What type of feedback does it provide? ▼

You receive feedback on response clarity, confidence levels, structural organization (such as STAR framework usage), and relevance to the question.
