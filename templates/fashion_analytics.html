PRIVACY & SAFETY POLICY • SECTION 12

Your Privacy & Data Dignity Come First

Ephemeral Processing

Uploaded images are analyzed in-session and never stored permanently without explicit consent.

Zero Public Exposure

Photographs are never exposed publicly, shared with third parties, or used for model retraining without consent.

Body-Positive Guidance

Recommendations are purely stylistic and proportion-based, avoiding judgmental descriptions of body shape or size.

Cosmetic Inspiration

Hair and lens suggestions are offered for aesthetic styling and do not constitute medical or dermatological advice.
