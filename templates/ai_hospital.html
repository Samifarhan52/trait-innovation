CLINICAL OVERSIGHT PIPELINE

Human Assistance & Escalation Workflow

HospitalAI includes a structured 5-step Human Assistance / Escalate to Staff workflow. When the AI cannot confidently answer a question or when a request requires hospital staff intervention, it follows these exact steps:

1

Step 1: Identify Need

Identify that human assistance is required due to query ambiguity or special hospital policies.

2

Step 2: Collect Details

Collect only the necessary enquiry details to ensure patient privacy and rapid resolution.

3

Step 3: Create & Route

Create and route the structured support request to the appropriate department queue.

4

Step 4: Transfer to Staff

Transfer context directly to the appropriate hospital team or duty desk personnel.

5

Step 5: User Confirmation

Provide the user with a transparent confirmation message, ticket ID, and expected response time.

Why Choose TRAIT HospitalAI?

TRAIT HospitalAI combines conversational AI with hospital-specific information and service workflows to help patients access information and assistance more easily while reducing repetitive enquiries handled manually by hospital staff.

The system is designed to support hospital staff and patient-service workflows, not replace medical professionals.

Important Healthcare Disclaimer

Mandatory Regulatory & Safety Policy

TRAIT HospitalAI must not be presented as a medical diagnosis or treatment system unless TRAIT specifically develops and validates such functionality.

WHAT HOSPITALAI PROVIDES:
  • Administrative assistance
  • Appointment assistance
  • Hospital / service information
  • Customer & visitor support
WHAT HOSPITALAI DOES NOT PROVIDE:
  • Medical diagnosis
  • Medical treatment decisions
  • Emergency medical advice

For emergencies, the system directs users to appropriate emergency services / hospital personnel rather than attempting to replace professional medical care.
