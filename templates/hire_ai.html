FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TRAIT HireAI? ▼

TRAIT HireAI is an HR technology solution covering candidate sourcing, training, assessment, matching, and recruitment workflows, connecting the entire talent lifecycle.

What stages of the talent lifecycle does TRAIT HireAI support? ▼

It supports sourcing, skill development/training, capability assessment, contextual matching, and collaborative recruitment workflows.

Does TRAIT HireAI replace human recruiters? ▼

No. TRAIT HireAI empowers recruiters with intelligent tools and data insights while keeping final hiring decisions entirely in human hands.
